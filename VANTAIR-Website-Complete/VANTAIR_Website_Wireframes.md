## 8. DESIGN GUIDELINES FOR B2B POSITIONING

- **Primary**: Professional Blue (#0066CC) - Trust & Credibility
- **Secondary**: Charcoal Gray (#2C2C2C) - Professional
- **Accent**: Vibrant Orange (#FF6B35) - Energy & Growth
- **Neutral**: Light Gray (#F5F5F5) - Clean backgrounds
- **Text**: Dark Gray (#333333) - Readability

### TYPOGRAPHY
- **Headlines**: Sans-serif Bold (Professional, Modern)
- **Body**: Sans-serif Regular (Clear, Accessible)
- **Minimum Font Size**: 16px (Mobile-friendly)

### KEY DESIGN ELEMENTS
1. **Trust Indicators**: Certifications, partner logos, testimonials
2. **Clear CTAs**: Multiple "Partnership Opportunity" buttons throughout
3. **Service Cards**: Consistent, scannable format
4. **Professional Imagery**: B2B networking, business professionals
5. **Data-Driven Content**: Statistics, metrics, case studies
6. **Mobile Responsive**: Optimized for tablet & mobile viewing

### UX BEST PRACTICES
- Quick navigation to partnership opportunities
- Easy-to-find contact information
- Clear differentiation between 9 service divisions
- Service/Partner search functionality
- FAQ section for common partnership questions
- Blog/Resources section for B2B insights
