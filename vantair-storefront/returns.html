Open Box Delivery Promise

Every Vantair order is delivered with open box inspection. You can check your product in front of the delivery agent before accepting. Not satisfied? Return it instantly — no forms, no waiting.

How It Works

Return Process

1

📦 Open Box Inspection

When your delivery agent arrives, you can open the package and inspect the product before providing your OTP. Take your time — the agent will wait.

2

🔍 Check for Defects

Check the product for any visible damage, leakage, incorrect item or quality issues. Compare with your order details if needed.

3

↩️ Instant Return (if defective)

If the product is damaged or defective, hand it back to the delivery agent immediately. Do not provide your OTP — the agent will mark it as a return. No additional steps required from you.

4

✅ Accept (if satisfied)

If you are happy with the product, provide your OTP to the delivery agent to confirm receipt. Once the OTP is accepted, the delivery is complete and the product cannot be returned.

5

💰 Refund Processed

For returned items, your refund is initiated within 1–2 business days and completed within 7 business days to your chosen refund method.

Important Notes

  • Returns are only accepted at the time of delivery during open box inspection
  • Once the Delivery OTP is provided and accepted, the order is considered delivered and no returns are accepted
  • Cosmetics and personal care products cannot be returned after use for hygiene reasons
  • For COD orders, refunds are issued as coupon codes credited to your dashboard
  • Orders cancelled before dispatch are eligible for a full refund to the original payment method
  • Refund coupons have no expiry date and can be used on any future Vantair order
