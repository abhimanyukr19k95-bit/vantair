Privacy Policy

Vantair ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose and safeguard your information when you visit our website www.vantair.in or make a purchase.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Personal identification: name, email address, phone number, profile photo
  • Delivery information: address, city, state, pincode
  • Order information: products purchased, payment method, order history
  • Account credentials: encrypted password (we never store plain-text passwords)
  • Communications: messages sent through our contact form

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and fulfil your orders
  • To send order confirmations, tracking updates and delivery notifications
  • To generate and verify your secure Delivery OTP
  • To manage your account and saved addresses
  • To respond to your enquiries and support requests
  • To send promotional communications (only if you have opted in)
  • To improve our website and product offerings

3. Delivery OTP & Security

Your Delivery OTP is generated specifically for each order to protect your delivery. It is password-protected, time-limited to 10 seconds of display, limited to 3 views maximum, and becomes invalid after successful delivery. We never share your OTP with any third party.

4. Data Sharing

We share your information only with:

  • Delivery partners (BlueDart, Shiprocket) — for order fulfilment only
  • Payment processors — for transaction processing (we do not store card or UPI details)
  • Legal authorities — when required by law

We never sell your personal information to third parties.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account information for as long as your account is active. Order history is retained for 7 years for legal and accounting purposes. You may request deletion of your account and data by contacting us.

6. Your Rights

  • Right to access your personal data
  • Right to correct inaccurate data
  • Right to delete your account and data
  • Right to opt out of marketing communications
  • Right to data portability

7. Contact for Privacy

For privacy-related enquiries: vantair@zohomail.in
