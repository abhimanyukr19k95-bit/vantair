Who We Are

A Brand Built on Quality & Trust

Vantair is a premium beauty and lifestyle brand headquartered in Kolkata, West Bengal. We develop and curate an exceptional range of perfumes, skincare, toiletries and cosmetics — all crafted to our exacting quality standards and proudly sold under the Vantair name.

Every product that carries the Vantair name has been developed, tested and approved by our team. We believe that quality should never be a luxury — it should be the standard.
